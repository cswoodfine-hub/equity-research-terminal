# G1 notes: readouts answered without a row

Registry records read from clinicaltrials.gov API v2 on 2026-10-28. One line per readout.

- **AZN Tagrisso, catalyst 307, NCT05629234 (ROSY-T)**: could not be established. A rollover (D5161N00007) for patients still benefiting from osimertinib monotherapy in an AstraZeneca parent study, condition "Cancer", safety primary endpoint, and it names no parent study. AstraZeneca's osimertinib programme on the registry includes solid tumour PK studies in renal and hepatic impairment (NCT02923947, NCT02161770), not only EGFR-mutated NSCLC, so NSCLC cannot be read off the record. AstraZeneca's list of the parent studies feeding ROSY-T would settle it.
- **JNJ Stelara, catalyst 973, NCT05092269**: two or more diseases at once. The official title calls it a "Basket, Long-term Extension Study" for children 2 to 17 rolling over from CNTO1275CRD1001 and CRD3004 (Crohn's disease), PUC3001 (ulcerative colitis) and JPA3001 (juvenile psoriatic arthritis), with safety-only primary endpoints. No single paediatric disease to put a row on.
- **MRK Keytruda, catalyst 426, NCT04976634 (MK-6482-016)**: a basket. Arm 1 enrols HCC, non-MSI-H CRC, PDAC, biliary tract, endometrial and ESCC; Arm 2 is IO-resistant ESCC only. Six diseases, primary endpoints DLT, AEs and ORR, no formal hypothesis testing. The protocol number is belzutifan's (MK-6482), and pembrolizumab is the backbone.
- **MRK Keytruda, catalyst 421, NCT02628067 (KEYNOTE-158)**: a basket. Ten tumour cohorts (anal, biliary, neuroendocrine, endometrial, cervical, vulvar, SCLC, mesothelioma, thyroid, salivary) plus tissue-agnostic MSI-H and TMB-H cohorts, one ORR primary endpoint across all of them.
- **MRK MK-2010, catalyst 1188, NCT07793942 (MK-2010-002)**: a basket. "Advanced solid tumors" with no tumour type in the eligibility. The arms pair MK-2010 (LM-299) with FOLFOX, belzutifan, gemcitabine and cisplatin, carboplatin and a taxane, and pemetrexed with a platin, so the arms cover several tumour types and none is named.
- **NVS Kymriah, catalyst 1047, NCT04094311 (CCTL019B2302)**: two diseases at once. An out-of-specification product study, ages 0 to 100, in Group A (paediatric and young adult r/r B-ALL), Group B (adult r/r LBCL) and Group C (Japan, adult r/r NHL), with an AE primary endpoint across all three groups.
- **PFE Xeljanz Xr, catalyst 494, NCT04624230**: not a readout of this asset. The arm is "tofacitinib 5 mg BID weight based adult equivalent", optionally 10 mg BID, which is the twice-daily Xeljanz tablet or oral solution, not the once-daily extended-release tablet. The shared label (set id 68e3d6b2-7838-4d2d-a417-09d919b43e13, effective 2026-07-13, read on api.fda.gov 2026-10-28) says "The safety and effectiveness of XELJANZ XR (extended-release tablets) in pediatric patients have not been established", and its paediatric approvals (PsA, pcJIA, from 2 years) are for the tablets and oral solution only. The readout belongs to Xeljanz, where data/paediatric_indications.csv already has its row (C536315, Pediatric ulcerative colitis, label_from_age 18). That row links only once the trial is mapped to Xeljanz, not Xeljanz Xr (data/trial_asset_map.csv).
- **ROG Lucentis, catalyst 513, NCT04657289 (Velodrome)**: not a readout of this asset. Both arms are the Port Delivery System refilled with ranibizumab 100 mg/mL every 36 or 24 weeks. That product is Susvimo, licensed under its own BLA761197 (label set id 9c6d9e2a-9a77-4d11-b692-de87cfde3444, nAMD after response to two anti-VEGF injections), not Lucentis's BLA125156. The book holds no Susvimo asset (no data/*.csv names one). In a Susvimo asset the disease would be Wet Macular Degeneration (D057135, checked on id.nlm.nih.gov 2026-10-28).
- **ROG Lucentis, catalyst 518, NCT05562947**: not a readout of this asset. The experimental arm is the PDS implant with ranibizumab 100 mg/mL (Susvimo, BLA761197). Lucentis, ranibizumab 0.5 mg intravitreal every 4 weeks, is the comparator arm, and that arm switches to the implant at Week 48. Same answer as Velodrome: Susvimo, in Wet Macular Degeneration (D057135).
- **ROG Perjeta, catalyst 509, NCT02320435 (MO29406)**: could not be established. An extension for patients in any Roche-sponsored pertuzumab parent study in solid tumours, alone or with other anti-cancer therapy, condition "Solid Tumors". It names no parent study, has no posted results, and pertuzumab's Roche programme spans more than breast cancer. The parent-study breakdown in MO29406's results, or the CTIS record 2023-505102-42-00, would settle it.
- **SNY Yf-Vax, catalyst 1245, NCT07103148 (VYF04)**: not a readout of this asset. The study tests vYF, Sanofi's investigational yellow fever vaccine, in healthy children aged 9 months to 5 years. Stamaril and YF-VAX are both active comparators ("compared with Stamaril vaccine and YF-VAX vaccine (both licensed vaccines)"), and every primary endpoint is vYF seroconversion against one of them. The readout belongs to vYF, which the book holds no asset for (no data/*.csv names it). It would be a paediatric yellow fever row (adult descriptor Yellow Fever, D015004), not approved at any age.

## Rows written with a judgement worth a second look

- AMGN Inebilizumab, NCT06570798: put on the SLE row. Inebilizumab's Subprotocol A is lupus nephritis (D008181), and a Lupus Nephritis row would be the narrower answer, at the cost of a second pair from one study.
- BMY Cobenfy, NCT07140913: put on Bipolar Disorder rather than Mania, because MeSH files Mania as a sign, not a disease.
- INCY INCA034176, NCT06585774 and NCT06821542: the registry's Bronchiolitis Obliterans Syndrome indexing builds a narrower pair than the population enrolled. If that pair exists on the asset, an exclusion may be wanted.
- LEGN LB2102, NCT05680922: on Small Cell Lung Carcinoma by its title. The inclusion criteria also admit LCNEC.
- NTLA nexiguran ziclumeran, NCT06128629 (ATTR-CM): C567782's preferred name says hereditary, and most ATTR-CM patients are wild-type. MeSH has no closer concept.
- ROG Columvi, NCT06047080: put on the existing Lymphoma, Non-Hodgkin row, not DLBCL (D016403), because a D016403 pair would drop the NHL row through drop_parent_of_child.
- MRK Keytruda, NCT03711032: put on Urinary Bladder Neoplasms, not Non-Muscle Invasive Bladder Neoplasms (D000093284), to keep it with the approved NMIBC indication.
