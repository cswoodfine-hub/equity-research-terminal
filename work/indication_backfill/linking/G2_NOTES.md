# G2 linking: readouts with no row

Checked 2026-10-28 against the registry (clinicaltrials.gov API v2), MeSH (id.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/lookup) and the FDA labels on api.fda.gov. 15 rows in G2_additions.csv cover 18 readouts; the 13 below have no row. No G2_paediatric.csv: no readout in this group needed a paediatric row.

## Several diseases at once

- ABBV Venclexta NCT03844048 (catalyst 579): an extension across indications. It enrols anyone dosed in an ongoing venetoclax study, across CLL, AML, multiple myeloma, non-Hodgkin lymphoma and ALL, with adverse events as the only primary outcome. No one disease.
- AZN Fasenra NCT06512883 (catalyst 674, CLIPS): enrols ages 6 to 17 only, but in two diseases, EGPA and hypereosinophilic syndrome, in one cohort with disease-specific criteria and safety and PK as the primary outcomes. (Label, set id da6aca1a-19ed-44a4-abb7-696c7d58b784, effective 2026-05-13: EGPA in adults only, HES from 12 years.) Agrees with work/indication_backfill/paediatric/NOTES.md.
- IPSC CNTY-101 NCT06255028 (catalyst 1000, CALiPSO-1): a basket. Four arms, one each for SLE, lupus nephritis, idiopathic inflammatory myopathies and diffuse cutaneous systemic sclerosis, Phase 1, 6 enrolled, with safety, DLTs and the Phase 2 regimen as primary outcomes.
- PFE Hympavzi NCT05611801 (catalyst 1154, BASIS KIDS): enrols ages 1 to 17 only, in severe hemophilia A or moderately severe to severe hemophilia B, one arm, with annualised bleeding rate across both. The book holds the two as separate rows. (Label, set id a2cc631e-13a6-40c2-acf9-065ccedfb90a, effective 2026-06-22: either disease from 6 years, so the layer would be ages 1 to 5.) What would settle it: a decision that one paediatric Hympavzi row covers both haemophilias, as the label does.
- ROG Enspryng NCT05503264 (catalyst 529, Cielo): a basket, by its own title. Two cohorts, NMDAR encephalitis (ages 12 and older) and LGI1 encephalitis (adults), each with its own primary outcome (mRS improvement at Week 24 and Week 52). MeSH holds them as two descriptors, Anti-N-Methyl-D-Aspartate Receptor Encephalitis (D060426) and Limbic Encephalitis (D020363), and has no descriptor for autoimmune encephalitis as a whole: Autoimmune Encephalitis is a narrower concept under Autoimmune Diseases of the Nervous System (D020274, MeSH SPARQL, checked 2026-10-28).
- ROG Rozlytrek NCT02568267 (catalyst 516, STARTRK-2): a tumour-agnostic basket across NTRK, ROS1 and ALK fusion-positive solid tumours, assigned to baskets by tumour type and fusion, one ORR primary outcome. No one disease.
- ROG Tecentriq NCT03148418 (catalyst 527, IMbrella A): an extension across indications for patients rolling over from any Genentech or Roche atezolizumab study, primary outcome continued access to treatment. No one disease.
- ROG Tecentriq NCT05112965 (catalyst 530, IMbrella C): the same design, with combination partners from bevacizumab to enzalutamide. No one disease.
- ROG Tecentriq NCT03768063 (catalyst 907, IMbrella B): the same design, with partners and comparators across lung, melanoma, prostate, ovarian and kidney regimens (alectinib, cobimetinib, vemurafenib, enzalutamide, niraparib, cabozantinib, sunitinib). No one disease.
- SNY Bivalent RSV+HMPV protein vaccine NCT07810010 (catalyst 1153): two co-primary outcomes, first RSV lower respiratory tract disease and first HMPV lower respiratory tract disease, in adults 60 and older. MeSH holds Respiratory Syncytial Virus Infections (D018357) apart from metapneumovirus infection, which has no descriptor of its own: Human MetapneumoVirus Infection is a narrower concept under Paramyxoviridae Infections (D018184, MeSH SPARQL, checked 2026-10-28). What would settle it: a decision to hold the bivalent vaccine on one combined row, or two rows with the readout priced on one.

## Not a readout of this asset

- INCY Jakafi NCT06804811 (catalyst 729) and NCT06548360 (catalyst 1162): both test ruxolitinib cream (INCB018424 phosphate cream, 0.75% and 1.5%) against vehicle cream in nonsegmental vitiligo, ages 6 to 11 and 2 to 11. That is Opzelura (topical, NDA215309, label set id 24da5509-6631-4795-9d42-273faecd08e7), not Jakafi (oral tablets, NDA202192 and NDA217180, label set id f1c82580-87ae-11e0-bc84-0002a5d5c51b, which approves no skin disease). data/paediatric_indications.csv already carries INCY Opzelura, Pediatric vitiligo, naming both studies, but the book maps the trials to Jakafi, so that row asserts nothing. What would settle it: a trial_asset_map override moving both studies to Opzelura; the existing row then links them. Jakafi's Dermatitis, Atopic and Hidradenitis Suppurativa rows look like cream studies mapped the same way and are worth the same check.
- ROG Tecentriq NCT05645692 (catalyst 517): atezolizumab is the comparator. The official title reads "Tobemstomig Alone or in Combination With Tiragolumab Versus Atezolizumab" in untreated platinum-ineligible urothelial cancer; Arm A is atezolizumab, Arms B and C are tobemstomig (RO7247669) with or without tiragolumab. The readout belongs to tobemstomig.

## Rows written with a judgement worth checking

- PRAX elsunersen NCT07019922: linked to the asset's existing Epilepsy row (D004827), as an ordinary addition rather than a paediatric row, following the call in work/indication_backfill/paediatric/NOTES.md (the registry's 1 day to 18 years admits an 18 year old, and early-onset SCN2A DEE has no adult population). Epilepsy is far broader than the disease; whoever seeds the row must hold the pool to early-onset SCN2A DEE. The alternative is a container concept like relutrigine's C567924, which would put elsunersen on relutrigine's SCN2A plus SCN8A pool.
- SNY Wayrilz: the asset carries no ITP row although ITP is its only FDA indication, so the row creates the pair, and the approved revenue sits on it.
- VRTX Journavx: DPN is written as a new pair, Diabetic Neuropathies (D003929), rather than on the asset's Pain row, because the label approves acute pain only.
- MRK MK-6916: the registry's only descriptor, Agnosia (D000377), is a mis-indexing of the condition "Pain Management"; the row is Postoperative Pain (D010149), MeSH's current label for what was Pain, Postoperative.
