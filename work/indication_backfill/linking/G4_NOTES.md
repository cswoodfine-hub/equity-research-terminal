# G4 notes: readouts with no row

Registry records read from clinicaltrials.gov API v2, checked 2026-10-28. One line per readout.

- **AZN Datroway, catalyst 684, NCT05489211 (TROPION-PanTumor03)**: reads out in several diseases at once. It is a Phase 2 master protocol with substudies 1 to 7: Dato-DXd alone or combined, in endometrial, gastric, castration-resistant prostate, ovarian, colorectal, urothelial and biliary tract cancer. Its primary outcomes are ORR across substudies, plus PSA50 for substudy 3 only and PFS for 4C only. No row.
- **AZN Lynparza, catalyst 298, NCT04421963 (ROSY-O)**: reads out in two diseases at once. It is a roll-over for patients from any AstraZeneca olaparib parent study, with conditions Ovarian Cancer and Breast Cancer and keywords naming HER2-negative metastatic and BRCA-mutated disease. Its only primary outcome is safety follow-up, with no disease-specific endpoint or cohort. No row.
- **AZN Saphnelo, catalyst 667, NCT06455449**: reads out in two diseases at once. It enrols adults with idiopathic inflammatory myopathy, either polymyositis or dermatomyositis (condition "Polymyositis, Dermatomyositis"), and has one pooled primary outcome, TIS of 40 or more, with no separate cohort for each disease. The asset carries both as separate rows (D017285, D003882). Pricing it would take a single Myositis (D009220) pair in place of the two, which is a change to the asset's pairs and not a link.
- **LEGN LB1908, catalyst 950, NCT05539430**: reads out in several diseases at once. This Phase 1 has cohorts MR1 and CR1 in gastric, GEJ or esophageal adenocarcinoma and cohort MR2 in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. Its primary outcomes are safety and dose finding across all cohorts, and the gastric and esophageal cohorts are already two of the asset's rows. No row.
- **LLY Mounjaro, catalyst 393 is linked (G4_additions.csv); catalyst 775, NCT06439277 (SURMOUNT-ADOLESCENTS-2)**: this is a Zepbound readout, not a Mounjaro one. It enrols ages 12 to 17 only, in obesity with weight-related comorbidities, and excludes type 2 diabetes and any HbA1c over 6.4%. Its primary outcomes are BMI change and a comorbidity composite. Obesity is on Zepbound's label and not on Mounjaro's (backend/brand_split.py). data/paediatric_indications.csv already names this study on LLY,Zepbound,D063766,Pediatric Obesity. That row attaches only when the trial is mapped to Zepbound, and the worklist shows it on Mounjaro. The fix is a trial_asset_map entry moving NCT06439277 to Zepbound. A Mounjaro paediatric row would value the same adolescents twice, so none is written.
- **LLY Omvoh, catalyst 778, NCT06937086**: this is not a readout of mirikizumab. Both arms get mirikizumab, and the randomised comparison is tirzepatide against placebo on top of it. The trial enrols adults with moderately to severely active UC and obesity, or overweight with a comorbidity, and excludes T1D and insulin-treated T2D. Its primary outcome is clinical remission together with at least 10% weight loss. The study belongs to tirzepatide, and to Zepbound, whose label covers obesity and overweight with comorbidity.
- **LLY Omvoh, catalyst 800, NCT06937099**: same design in Crohn's disease. Both arms get mirikizumab and only tirzepatide or placebo is randomised. The primary outcome is CDAI clinical remission, endoscopic remission and at least 10% weight loss. The study belongs to tirzepatide (Zepbound), not Omvoh.
- **PRAX vormatrigine, catalyst 1237, NCT07287163**: reads out in two diseases at once. This open-label extension takes completers of PRAX-628-212 (focal seizures) and PRAX-628-321 and other vormatrigine trials. Its conditions are Epilepsy, Tonic-Clonic, Focal Seizure and Primary Generalized Seizure, and every primary outcome is safety "in adults with focal or primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures". No row.
- **SANA SC291, catalyst 957, NCT06294236 (GLEAM)**: reads out in several diseases at once. This Phase 1 has three cohorts, lupus nephritis, extrarenal SLE and ANCA-associated vasculitis (GPA or MPA), with one primary outcome, safety and dose-limiting toxicity, across them. No row.

## Linked, with doubts (rows in G4_additions.csv)

- **ABBV NOA LIFT (NCT07794878)**: MeSH has no disease concept for nasolabial folds. The row uses Nasolabial Fold (D060052), an anatomy descriptor (A01), because it names exactly what is treated. Skin Aging (D015595) is the broader alternative. If the population model reads only disease descriptors, this needs a decision.
- **ROG PDS Implant (NCT04108156)**: MeSH has no DME descriptor. The row uses Macular Edema (D008269), not Diabetic Retinopathy (D003930), which the registry gives most DME studies. The Susvimo label lists DME and DR as separate indications. The asset may duplicate a Susvimo asset already on the book.
- **NVS Fabhalta (NCT04817618)**: the study enrols ages 12 to 60, and the pending primary completion is the adolescent cohort. The label approves C3G in adults only. It is linked to the adult row because the study is not children-only, but the remaining readout is in fact adolescent.
- **PRAX vormatrigine (NCT07505004)**: linked to the asset's Seizures row, where POWER1 sits, rather than to a new Epilepsies, Partial (D004828) row, so that one focal-epilepsy population is not held twice.
- **MRK Lipfendra (NCT07821944)**: the title says hyperlipidemia. It is linked to Hypercholesterolemia, following the Lipfendra label and the CORALreef Lipids design.
- **MRK ULO and GILD GS-3242**: both rows create HIV Infections pairs. The MK-8527 row in data/asset_indication_additions.csv notes that HIV Infections already carries the diagnosed population another asset is valued on, so the population guard should be checked when these are priced.
- **AUTL Aucatzyl (NCT07053800)**: the study enrols ages 12 to 65, so adolescents and adults together. The label (adult B-ALL only) covers neither in lupus.
