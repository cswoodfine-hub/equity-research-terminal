# G3 notes: readouts with no row, and rows to check

Registry records read from clinicaltrials.gov API v2 on 2026-10-28.

## No row

- AZN | Imfinzi | catalyst 321 | NCT05303532: no row, not a readout in one disease. ROSY-D is a rollover sub-study of AstraZeneca's core rollover protocol. It enrols any patient benefiting from durvalumab in any completed AstraZeneca parent oncology study (condition "Cancer", registry MeSH Neoplasms only), with serious adverse events as the primary outcome. It spans every tumour the parents studied, so no single disease can be chosen.
- JNJ | IMBRUVICA | catalyst 978 | NCT01804686: no row, an extension across several diseases. PCI-32765CAN3001 is a long-term extension for patients from any completed ibrutinib study, alone or with nivolumab (CLL/SLL, mantle cell, follicular, DLBCL, Waldenstrom, chronic GVHD). The primary outcome is adverse events, and the registry indexes all five of the asset's rows.
- LLY | Foundayo | catalyst 585 | NCT06993792: no row, two diseases at once. GZPO is the master protocol that sends participants to GZP1 (NCT06972459, obesity or overweight without type 2 diabetes) or to GZP2 (NCT06972472, type 2 diabetes). It has no eligibility criteria of its own, and its primary outcome is "Number of Participants Allocated to Each Study". The readouts are the two sub-studies. GZP2 is linked to Diabetes Mellitus, Type 2 in G3_additions.csv, and GZP1 is not in this worklist.
- ROG | Gazyva | catalyst 899 | NCT06084936: no row, not Gazyva's readout. The study tests glofitamab monotherapy against bendamustine-rituximab or rituximab-lenalidomide in relapsed or refractory mantle cell lymphoma after a BTK inhibitor. Obinutuzumab appears only as the two pretreatment infusions before glofitamab ("Participants will receive two intravenous (IV) obinutuzumab pretreatments prior to receiving IV glofitamab"). The readout belongs to Columvi (glofitamab), which the book carries (data/assumptions/rog_columvi.csv). There its disease would be Lymphoma, Mantle-Cell (D020522). The trial-to-asset mapping, not this file, should move it.

## Rows written with a judgement worth checking

- GILD | Sunlenca | NCT07047716 (Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis): the study is lenacapavir for PrEP. Gilead sells that as Yeztugo, which the book holds only as a company line (data/company_lines/gild.csv), while the trial is mapped to the lenacapavir asset named Sunlenca. The row follows that mapping. If Yeztugo becomes its own asset, the readout and this pair move to it.
- ALNY | Amvuttra | NCT06679946 (Amyloidosis): the extension enrols ATTR-CM, wild-type and hereditary. I chose Amyloidosis over the hereditary SCR C567782 because HELIOS-A (polyneuropathy) carries C567782 and HELIOS-B (cardiomyopathy) alone carries D000686. The study is a safety extension: its primary outcome is adverse events.
- ABBV | Elahere | NCT05445778 (Carcinoma, Ovarian Epithelial): epithelial ovarian, primary peritoneal and fallopian tube cancer are enrolled as one population. I took the narrowest row. Ovarian Neoplasms is the alternative if that is the row the book prices.
- MRK | MK-7262 and NVS | DII235 (Hyperlipoproteinemias, D006951): MeSH has no descriptor for elevated Lp(a). Its supplementary concept C563617 maps to D006951. The book's Lp(a) outcome programmes sit on Cardiovascular Diseases (olpasiran, pelacarsen) and Atherosclerosis (lepodisiran). These two are Lp(a)-lowering Phase 2s, so I used the lipoprotein disease rather than the outcome. A seed on this row should carry the elevated Lp(a) pool only.
- VRTX | VX-147 (Renal Insufficiency, Chronic, D051436): MeSH has no concept for APOL1-mediated kidney disease. CKD follows the ZENITH precedent, but the descriptor's population is far larger than AMKD, so the seed must narrow it to the APOL1 high-risk genotype. FSGS (D005923) is too narrow, because AMPLITUDE enrols proteinuric disease beyond FSGS. The study enrols ages 10 to 65 together and the drug has no approval, so I wrote no paediatric row.
- CABA | CABA-201 | NCT06154252 (Myositis): the title has "Juvenile", but the study enrols adults (18 to 75) and children (6 to 17) together. CABA-201 has no label, so the readout decides the IIM indication itself. The additions row links it to Myositis, and I wrote no paediatric row.
- ARGX | Vyvgart Hytrulo | NCT05979441 and CABA-201 both go on Myositis, not Dermatomyositis. Each protocol's disease is idiopathic inflammatory myopathy across subtypes, and MeSH lists Idiopathic Inflammatory Myopathy as an entry term of Myositis.
- ARGX | Vyvgart Hytrulo | NCT04812925 assumes the trial is mapped to Vyvgart Hytrulo, as the worklist prints it. If it is mapped to Vyvgart, the row does not apply (curated rows require the trial on the named asset).
