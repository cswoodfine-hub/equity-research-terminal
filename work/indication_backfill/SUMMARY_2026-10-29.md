## Engine fixes, each tested

- The gate nets a layer's overlap with the approved business (Imfinzi bladder was staked
  at 2,080mm where the forecast adds 125mm).
- A layer on a product modelled from patients is risked at its own odds, and only a row
  the research marks as a layer counts as one (Lipfendra's whole product was staked on a
  safety study).
- A layer may state its own price and discontinuation (Journavx's neuropathy layer).
- A curated addition links the studies it names; a study named on two rows decides neither.
- A second readout on a priced layer reads "same gate later", not "no gate".
- An already-approved layer says so before asking for a pool.
- The asset merge no longer fails when both rows hold a readout on the same pair.

## What is left unpriced, and why (141 at no_gate, 21 no forecast)

| reason | catalysts |
|---|---|
| already approved for these patients: the readout adds no one | 50 |
| the study spans several diseases at once, or tests another drug | 32 |
| no model possible: no filed figure, held, on clinical hold, or the product's row exists only in the book | 31 |
| a layer whose price or pool cannot be sourced, or not an efficacy readout | 16 |
| the programme failed or stopped | 7 |
| no forecast: no US route, stopped, unidentified candidate, no price | 21 |

None of these is priced by inventing a number. The ones that could move with more facts:

- **No published peak**: Aucatzyl, Yeztugo, Niktimvo, Penmenvy, Datroway. Their readouts
  that matter already price; only the lines themselves wait on a peak.
- **No filed product figure**: Emrelis, Augtyro, Balversa, Litfulo, Veopoz, Menquadfi,
  Hyrnuo, Imaavy.
- **Held** (`held/HELD.md`): Pavblu (inside Amgen's other-products line), Inlexzo's failed
  study rows.
- **Book-only names**: etavopivat, LY4515100, BMS-986489, ivonescimab and the VAX-31 rows
  should price in the book, where their target rows exist.
