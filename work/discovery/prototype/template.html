What to trust

  • The translation ranking ties NIH iCite's published score, which sees citations this one does not.
  • The labs ranking is the strong one for company activity. The top tenth of lab heads held 46% of those who went on to a company record.
  • The authors' record does most of the work. A paper's own words add little for company activity.

What not to trust yet

  • "None seen yet" means none in three sources. Founders with an advisory seat and under 25% leave no trace in them, so well-known founders sit in that group.
  • Records are matched by name and state or town. Some are the wrong person, and some real ones are dropped.
  • The UK register shows owners of 25% or more of live companies only. The UK ranking is no better than a count.
  • Patents are not in the record. They need a USPTO key.
Sources: Europe PMC (papers), OpenAlex (authors and institutions), NIH RePORTER (small-business awards), SEC Form D data sets, Companies House (company and ownership files), NIH iCite (clinical citations), PubMed (conflict-of-interest statements).
