Sources: Europe PMC, OpenAlex, NIH RePORTER, SEC Form D data sets, Companies House, NIH iCite, PubMed. Company records are matched to authors by name and state or town, and are not verified. Scores come from a word-frequency and rule model; no language model wrote or scored anything here.

How reliable is this?

Each bar is the share of the top 5% of a list that had the outcome within three years. The model was trained on 2017 and 2018 and scored on 2019 and 2020.

What to trust
  • The lab ranking is the strong one. The top tenth of lab heads held 46% of those who went on to a company record.
  • "Closest to patients" ties NIH iCite's published score, which sees citations this one does not.
  • A lab's record does most of the work. A paper's own words add little for company activity.
What not to trust yet
  • "No company record seen" means none in three sources. A founder with an advisory seat and under 25% leaves no trace in them.
  • Records are matched by name and state or town. Some are the wrong person, and some real ones are dropped.
  • The UK register shows only owners of 25% or more of live companies, so UK labs are undercounted.
  • Patents are not included yet.
