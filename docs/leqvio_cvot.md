# Leqvio (inclisiran, NVS) — pricing the VICTORION-2-PREVENT readout

Research date: 2026-11-11. All sources free and publicly reachable. Nothing written to `data/` or the DB.

---

## VERDICT

**No. There is no citable peak-sales figure for Leqvio that is conditional on the cardiovascular outcomes trial.**

Three separate things are true, and together they close the question:

1. **Novartis has never published a peak-sales number for Leqvio alone** — not in a 20-F, not in a 6-K, not in a media release, not in an investor deck. It names Leqvio only inside a group. The FY2025 20-F contains the string "peak sales" **zero times**. The Nov 2025 guidance release names individual figures for Kisqali (USD 10bn+) and Scemblix (USD 4bn+) and puts Leqvio only in the eight-asset "USD 3-10 billion" band. The book's existing note on this is correct and I could not improve on it.

2. **No third-party peak estimate is framed as conditional on the CVOT.** The two free third-party figures I could find — GlobalData's USD 3.4bn in 2030 (the one the book already uses) and GlobalData's earlier USD 2.5bn by 2027 — are both unconditional market forecasts, and both predate the current trial timetable. The USD 2.5bn piece actually discusses ORION-4 and explicitly declines to make the forecast depend on it.

3. **The widely repeated "Novartis has pegged Leqvio peak sales at above $4 billion" is not a Novartis statement.** It is FiercePharma's own unattributed characterisation (Angus Liu, 21 Jul 2026). The Narasimhan quote in that same article contains no number. **Do not put USD 4bn in the book as a Novartis figure.** See Q1 below for the exact text.

### Two things that matter more than the missing number

**(a) A Novartis peak-sales update lands in seven days.** Novartis's Q3 2026 deck (27 Oct 2026, p.26) announces Meet Novartis Management, **London, 18-19 November 2026**, agenda item: "Updated peak sales potential for key growth drivers". Leqvio is one of the eight. If a Leqvio-alone figure is ever going to exist, this is the most likely moment. The book should wait for it rather than invent one.

**(b) VICTORION-2P is not the first CVOT to read out — ORION-4 is, and it may already have.** Novartis's own Q3 2026 deck says the ORION-4 readout (NCT03705234, n=16,124, 3P/4P-MACE composite, same drug, same secondary-prevention setting) is **anticipated in Q4 2026** — i.e. now. As recently as 19 Aug 2026 Novartis was saying both trials would read out in 2027; between August and October it pulled ORION-4 forward. A book that prices "VICTORION-2P succeeds in 2027" as the gate is pricing the *second* card to turn over. Related and uncomfortable context: on 7 Nov 2026 Novartis abandoned **pelacarsen** in ASCVD after Lp(a)HORIZON (n=8,322) showed no MACE benefit despite large Lp(a) lowering — a different mechanism, but a Novartis lipid-lowering CVOT that failed four days ago, and the reason several outlets are now questioning the house's CV strategy.

## 2. Novartis statements on what VICTORION-2-PREVENT does commercially

Novartis is consistently careful here. It describes V2P as evidence-generation and never states a commercial consequence — no label claim promised, no guideline position claimed, no primary-care or payer argument made conditional on it. The three closest statements:

### (a) The Q3 2026 deck — the single most useful line in this whole file

> "US label updated with cardiovascular outcomes statement; ORION-4 and V2P readouts anticipated in Q4 and 2027, respectively"

- Novartis Q3 Results investor presentation, **27 October 2026**, slide 10 (Leqvio sales evolution slide, headed "Leqvio® grew +63% cc in Q3, reflecting strong demand around the world").
- https://www.novartis.com/sites/novartis_com/files/q3-2026-investor-presentation.pdf

Same slide, the commercial framing Novartis actually uses today — note it rests on persistency and channel, not outcomes:
> "MOTRx growth (+60%) ahead of market (+46%) and main competitor (+46%), reflecting Leqvio's differentiated persistency"
> "Continued improvement in Medicare NBRx share (+4.9%pts to 22%)"
> "Increasing depth in priority health systems (+28% vs. PY), with continued focus on post-event patients and increasing access/acquisition confidence"

### (b) The August 2026 label-update page

> "Novartis continues to advance the VictORION clinical trial program, one of the largest clinical trial programs of its kind, with over 60,000 patients enrolled across more than 30 trials in more than 50 countries. The program includes ORION-4 and VICTORION-2-PREVENT, two major secondary prevention cardiovascular outcomes trials expected to read out in 2027. Novartis remains committed to advancing cardiovascular disease prevention and addressing the unmet needs of patients at increased cardiovascular risk."

- Novartis US, "FDA updates LEQVIO® label", **19 August 2026**.
- https://www.novartis.com/us-en/news/fda-updates-leqvio-label

Note: this page says **both** trials read out in 2027. The 27 Oct 2026 deck moved ORION-4 to Q4 2026. The timetable shifted inside ten weeks.

### (c) The catalyst slide

Q3 2026 deck p.17, "Multiple readouts still expected in 2026, with a strong catalyst path in 2027":
- Readouts **2026**: "Leqvio® CVRR-LDLC (ORION-4)"
- Readouts **2027**: "Leqvio® CVRR-LDLC (V2P)"

### Adjacent, not conditional: the guideline position already happened

> "Leqvio now included in updated 2026 ACC/AHA guidelines, highlighting criticality of aggressive lipid management, adherence and patient preference, including for less frequent dosing"

- Novartis Q1 Results investor presentation, **28 April 2026**, slide 9.
- https://www.novartis.com/sites/novartis_com/files/q1-2026-investor-presentation.pdf

This matters for the book's gate logic: the guideline inclusion Novartis might have been expected to claim as V2P upside has **already been obtained without outcomes data**. That shrinks the V2P delta rather than widening it.

## 4. The current FDA label's cardiovascular claim status

**Confirmed: inclisiran carries NO outcomes claim of its own. The label's CV sentence is a class statement that explicitly names statins and monoclonal-antibody PCSK9 inhibitors — and not siRNA.**

Source: openFDA `https://api.fda.gov/drug/label.json?search=openfda.generic_name:"inclisiran"`.
Set ID **6fc0afca-4513-4c35-b594-6544aee29a44**, **version 13**, `effective_time` **20260819**.

Section 1, Indications and Usage, verbatim:

> "LEQVIO® is indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to reduce low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) in: adults with hypercholesterolemia. adults and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older with heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH). pediatric patients aged 12 years and older with homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HoFH). **Cardiovascular outcomes trials have demonstrated that reducing LDL-C lowers the risk for major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) in adults at increased risk when treated with statins or monoclonal antibody proprotein convertase subtilisin kexin type 9 (PCSK9) inhibitors as an add-on to statin therapy.**"

The load-bearing words are "when treated with statins or monoclonal antibody PCSK9 inhibitors". Inclisiran is an siRNA. The sentence sits in inclisiran's label and describes *other agents'* outcomes evidence.

Section 14 Clinical Studies describes only ORION-9/-10/-11, all LDL-C percent-change endpoints ("The difference between the LEQVIO and placebo groups in mean percentage change in LDL-C from baseline to Day 510 was -52%"). No MACE endpoint anywhere outside the class sentence. `recent_major_changes`: "Indications and Usage (1) 02/2026; Contraindications (4) 02/2026; Warnings and Precautions (5.1) 02/2026".

**When the class sentence entered:** DailyMed version history for this set ID shows v13 published **21 Aug 2026** (label revision date 19 Aug 2026), v12 **16 Feb 2026**, v11 8 Aug 2025, v10 20 Jun 2025. Novartis announced the addition on 19 Aug 2026 and booked it as a Q3 2026 development:

> "FDA updated the Leqvio label to include language recognizing that lowering LDL-C reduces the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) in adults at increased cardiovascular risk. This update reflects established evidence from statins and monoclonal antibody PCSK9 inhibitors, supporting LDL-C reduction as a key component of cardiovascular risk management."

- Novartis Q3 2026 media release, **27 October 2026**, R&D update table.
- https://www.novartis.com/sites/novartis_com/files/q3-2026-media-release-en.pdf

Novartis's own 19 Aug 2026 page is explicit that this is borrowed evidence, not inclisiran's:

> "This update reflects the established scientific evidence from statins or monoclonal antibody proprotein convertase subtilisin kexin type 9 (PCSK9) inhibitors as an add-on to statin therapy, supporting LDL-C reduction as an important component of cardiovascular risk management. It also provides relevant context for the treatment of hypercholesterolemia. Novartis believes this reinforces the importance of achieving and maintaining recommended LDL-C levels in patients at increased cardiovascular risk."

So the book's existing record is right, with one correction worth making: the class statement is **recent** (Aug 2026), not long-standing, and Novartis treated it as a win worth announcing. That is itself a signal about how much the company thinks the CV sentence is worth commercially.

### What this means for the book

Submission in 2027, event-driven. A US sNDA/sBLA review of 10-12 months puts a **label carrying inclisiran's own outcomes claim in 2028 at the earliest**, and that inference is mine, not Novartis's — the company states no approval year. If the book wants to model a revenue inflection, the gate opens at submission in 2027 and the *claim* arrives later.

Also relevant: the 2027 filing is supported by **two** trials, and Novartis has said ORION-4 reads out first (Q4 2026). A 2027 filing is therefore not strictly contingent on V2P alone.

## Recommendation for the book

Do not create a "VICTORION-2P succeeds" peak number. There is nothing to cite it to. Three honest options, in order of preference:

1. **Wait seven days.** Meet Novartis Management, 18-19 Nov 2026, explicitly promises "Updated peak sales potential for key growth drivers". Leqvio is one of the eight. This is the highest-probability moment for a Leqvio-alone figure to come into existence, and it is a week away.
2. **Price the gate, not the level.** The book already notes, correctly, that the CVOT changes the claim and the payer case rather than the eligible population, and that it belongs in the marketed line's growth rate. That remains the defensible treatment. The readout can be carried as a named, unpriced catalyst with a date.
3. If a scenario is demanded anyway, state plainly in the scenario's own source field that **no conditional peak figure exists** and that the scenario's ceiling is an analyst judgement, not a sourced number — the same discipline the book applies elsewhere.

Two corrections to the existing file are supported by this research and do not require any new number:
- The class statement in the label is **new as of 19 Aug 2026** (SPL v13), not long-standing — and Novartis announced it as a win. Worth recording, because it slightly reduces the V2P delta.
- The guideline position (2026 ACC/AHA inclusion) was **already obtained without outcomes data**, per Novartis's own Q1 2026 deck. Also reduces the V2P delta.
- And the headline risk: **ORION-4, not V2P, is the first CVOT to read out** — Novartis's own deck says Q4 2026.
