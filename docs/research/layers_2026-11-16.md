## Three things to pass on beyond the rows

1. **Two assets sit under the wrong company.** `Palsonify` (asset 3518, paltusotine) is
   Crinetics Pharmaceuticals', not Vertex's; `Pombiliti` (asset 3435, cipaglucosidase alfa)
   is Amicus Therapeutics', not BioMarin's. Both are confirmed by DailyMed labeller and by
   the lead sponsor on their own catalyst's study. Writing layers for either would have put
   another company's rNPV inside a Vertex or BioMarin sum of the parts.
2. **The VAX-31 alias merge has not taken.** `data/asset_alias_map.csv` maps
   `0.5 mL of the low dose VAX-31` to `31 valent pneumococcal conjugate vaccine`
   ("One molecule, one row (2026-10-13)"), but asset 2847 still exists with zero
   assumptions, there is no asset by the target name in `assets` at all, and the catalyst is
   still attached to 2847 — which is why it reports "the indication's own assumptions"
   instead of inheriting `pcvx_vax_31.csv`.
3. **The VYJUVEK incidence row is internally inconsistent with its own prevalence by about
   2.7x, and the direction is certain.** The registry incidence (24/yr) is coherent with the
   registry's own prevalence (1,109) but the book carries Krystal's 3,000. The consequence
   is a layer that peaks in year five and then declines, which under-prices. The reconciled
   alternative, 65/yr, is a derivation rather than a source and is named in the row's note
   as the replacement if the book ever decides one disease must carry one population on both
   cells.
