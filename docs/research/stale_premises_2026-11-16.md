# Stale premises check — three catalyst rows

Verification date: 2026-11-16. All sources below are free and publicly reachable. Every
assertion is tied to a named, dated primary document.

---

## 1. COCOON — NCT06120140 — J&J, Rybrevant + Lazcluze, NSCLC

**VERDICT: CONFIRMED — the readout has already happened. The book is pricing a past event.**

### J&J's own announcement

Johnson & Johnson media centre, dateline **RARITAN, N.J., MARCH 20, 2025**:
<https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/rybrevant-amivantamab-vmjw-plus-lazcluze-lazertinib-significantly-outperforms-standard-of-care-in-first-line-egfr-mutated-lung-cancer-with-compelling-new-data-at-elcc-2025>

Sub-headline, verbatim:

> "Preventative dermatologic regimen meets primary endpoint and enhances patient experience"

Body, verbatim:

> "First presentation of data from the Phase 2 COCOON study evaluating an easy-to-use,
> readily available prophylactic regimen for the prevention of dermatologic reactions in
> the first-line treatment of EGFR-mutant advanced NSCLC in patients receiving RYBREVANT®
> plus LAZCLUZE™ (Mini Oral Abstract #10MO)."

The 2025-03-20 piece is the congress-preview release. The numbers landed a week later in
the J&J Innovative Medicine EMEA release, dateline **BEERSE, BELGIUM (27 March 2025)**,
"Enhanced preventive regimen demonstrates a statistically significant and clinically
meaningful reduction in dermatologic reactions in patients with EGFR-mutated advanced
NSCLC", Abstract #10MO:
<https://www.jnj.com/innovativemedicine/emea/enhanced-preventive-regimen-demonstrates-a-statistically-significant-and-clinically-meaningful-reduction-in-dermatologic-reactions-in-patients-with-egfr-mutated-advanced-nsclc>

> "By Week 12, patients on the COCOON regimen had significantly fewer Grade 2 or higher
> dermatologic events compared to standard of care management (39 percent vs 77 percent;
> odds ratio [OR], 0.19; 95 percent confidence interval [CI], 0.09–0.40; P<0.0001)"

...described as "meeting the pre-specified endpoint of the study." Presented at the 2025
European Lung Cancer Congress, 27 March 2025.

### Peer-reviewed primary publication (the stronger citation)

Journal of Thoracic Oncology, online 2025-09-09, Oct 2025 issue. PMID 40923969,
DOI 10.1016/j.jtho.2025.07.117. "Enhanced Versus Standard Dermatologic Management With
Amivantamab-Lazertinib in EGFR-Mutated Advanced NSCLC: The COCOON Global Randomized
Controlled Trial." Abstract, verbatim:

> "Primary end point was incidence of grade 2 or higher dermatologic AEs of interest
> (DAEIs) by week 12."

> "In total, 201 participants were randomized (99 to COCOON DM and 102 to SoC DM). At a
> median follow-up of 7.1 months, COCOON DM demonstrated significant reduction in the
> primary end point versus SoC DM (42% versus 75%; OR, 0.24; 95% confidence interval,
> 0.13-0.45; p < 0.0001)."

The DataBank field of the PubMed record registers the trial as ClinicalTrials.gov
NCT06120140 — same study.

### Why ClinicalTrials.gov still looks open

NCT06120140 (API v2, record last updated 2026-11-03) reads overallStatus
ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING, primaryCompletionDate 2027-05-31 (ESTIMATED), enrolment 305
(ACTUAL), no resultsSection. That is not a contradiction. The registry enrolment of 305
spans the randomised main study **plus** four later add-ons that are still running:

- "Sub-study: Cohort A: Ruxolitinib" (reactive treatment of Grade >=2 DAEIs)
- "Sub-study: Cohort B: Tacrolimus"
- "Amivantamab Subcutaneous (SC) Expansion Cohort: Standard Schedule"
- "Amivantamab SC Expansion Cohort: Modified Schedule"

Only Arms A and B (201 randomised) carry the primary endpoint, and those have read out,
been presented and been published. The 2027-05-31 date belongs to the expansion cohorts.

**Pricing implication:** the row is pricing a catalyst that resolved on 2025-03-27 and was
published 2025-09-09. There is no dermatologic-prophylaxis readout left to price; any
remaining optionality is the SC expansion cohorts and the ruxolitinib/tacrolimus
sub-studies, which are a different and much smaller question.

## 2. Hyrnuo (sevabertinib) — NCT06452277 (SOHO-02) — Bayer, NSCLC

**VERDICT: CONFIRMED on every element — accelerated approval, NDA 219972/S-001,
2026-09-09, SOHO-02 named as the confirmatory trial, FDA clock 04/2029.**

### Approval record (openFDA drugsfda, data last_updated 2026-11-13)

`https://api.fda.gov/drug/drugsfda.json?search=application_number:"NDA219972"`

- Sponsor BAYER HEALTHCARE; brand HYRNUO; generic SEVABERTINIB.
- ORIG-1: submission_status AP, submission_status_date **20251119**, PRIORITY, Type 1 NME,
  Orphan.
- SUPPL-1: submission_type SUPPL, submission_number 1, submission_status **AP**,
  submission_status_date **20260909**, PRIORITY, submission_class_code EFFICACY.

So the asserted "NDA 219972/S-001, 2026-09-09" is exactly right.

### Indication wording (openFDA label, SPL version 10, effective_time 20260917)

`https://api.fda.gov/drug/label.json?search=openfda.brand_name:"HYRNUO"`
SPL set id d7a11334-6e7a-445b-9a16-bdcd9acbe61f. Section 1, verbatim:

> "HYRNUO is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or
> metastatic non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumors have HER2
> (ERBB2) tyrosine kinase domain (TKD) activating mutations, as detected by an
> FDA-authorized test [see Dosage and Administration (2.1)]. This indication is approved
> under accelerated approval based on objective response rate (ORR) and duration of
> response (DOR) [see Clinical Studies (14)]. Continued approval for this indication may
> be contingent upon verification and description of clinical benefit in a confirmatory
> trial."

Recent Major Changes: "Indications and Usage (1) 09/2026". **Accelerated, not traditional.**

The indication carries no prior-therapy restriction, so it covers first line. Section 14.1
makes the first-line basis explicit — SOHO-01 (NCT05099172) Group F, treatment-naive,
N=69, ORR 75% (95% CI 64, 85), DOR >=6 months 73%, DOR >=12 months 38%.

The label itself does **not** name the confirmatory trial or give a date — it only uses the
generic "a confirmatory trial" boilerplate. The naming and the date are in the approval
letter.

### Approval letter — the confirmatory trial and the clock

FDA approval letter, NDA 219972/S-001, dated 2026-09-10 (document date 20260910),
Reference ID 5865843:
<https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2026/219972Orig1s001ltr.pdf>

Header reads "NDA 219972/S-001 / **ACCELERATED APPROVAL**". Verbatim:

> "It is approved under accelerated approval pursuant to section 506(c) of the FD&C Act and
> 21 CFR 314.510, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the
> enclosed agreed-upon labeling."

Under ACCELERATED APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS, PMR 4933-1, verbatim:

> "4933-1 Complete the ongoing multicenter, randomized clinical trial, Study SOHO-02,
> intended to verify and describe the clinical benefit of sevabertinib in adult patients
> with locally advanced or metastatic non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer whose tumors
> have HER2 (ERBB2) tyrosine kinase domain activating mutations and who have not received
> prior therapy for advanced disease."

> "The timetable you submitted on October 23, 2025, states that you will conduct this trial
> according to the following schedule:
> Trial Completion: **04/2029**
> Final Report Submission: 10/2029"

(The other PMRs, 4933-2 hepatic impairment and 4933-3 MATE1/MATE2-K DDI, are 505(o) safety
studies, not the confirmatory trial.)

### SOHO-02 registry record

NCT06452277 (API v2, updated 2026-11-04): Bayer, acronym SOHO-02, Phase 3, open-label
randomized active-controlled, first-line, enrolment 444 ESTIMATED, status RECRUITING,
primaryCompletionDate **2027-10-28 (ESTIMATED)**, completionDate 2029-06-27.

**Pricing implication:** the row cannot be priced as a 2027 binary approval event. The
first-line indication is already granted (2026-09-09) and already being sold; SOHO-02 is a
Subpart H confirmatory conversion. The 2027-10-28 registry primary completion is the data
cut, but FDA's accountability clock is Trial Completion 04/2029 / final report 10/2029.
The economically meaningful risk is withdrawal/conversion, not approval — a different
payoff shape and a different date.

## 3. PENMENVY / MenABCWY — NCT05087056 — GSK

**VERDICT: CONFIRMED — Phase 2b, n=305 actual, two arms (0/24-month and 0/48-month
MenABCWY), no 0/6-month active comparator, descriptive primary endpoints.**

Source: ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 record for NCT05087056, statusVerifiedDate 2026-09,
record last updated 2026-09-28. <https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05087056>

### Phase

officialTitle, verbatim:

> "A Phase IIb, Randomized, Observer-Blind Study to Describe the Safety, Tolerability, and
> Immunogenicity of MenABCWY Administered on Different Dosing Schedules in Healthy
> Adolescents"

designModule.phases = ["PHASE2"]. Sponsor GlaxoSmithKline, org study ID 215344,
EudraCT 2021-001670-33.

### Enrolment

enrollmentInfo: count **305**, type **ACTUAL**.

### Arm structure — this is the load-bearing finding

Two arms only, and both receive MenABCWY. Verbatim:

> "ABCWY-24 Group" — "Participants receive 2 doses of the MenABCWY vaccine at Day 1 and
> Day 721, 1 dose of Placebo at Day 1441."

> "ABCWY-48 Group" — "Participants receive 2 doses of the MenABCWY vaccine at Day 1 and
> Day 1441, 1 dose of Placebo at Day 721."

Intervention description, verbatim:

> "Two doses of the MenABCWY vaccine, administered intramuscularly in the deltoid region of
> the non-dominant arm, on a 0-, 24-month schedule in the ABCWY-24 Group, and a 0-,
> 48-month schedule in the ABCWY-48 Group."

The placebo is a single saline filler dose used to keep the observer blind — it is not a
comparator arm. **There is no 0/6-month arm anywhere in the record.** The schedules under
study are 0/24-month and 0/48-month.

### Primary outcome measures (verbatim, 7 of them)

1. "Percentage of participants with human serum bactericidal assay (hSBA) titers ≥ lower
   limit of quantitation (LLOQ) for each N. meningitidis serogroup B indicator strains"
   — time frame "At Baseline (Day 1)"
2. "Percentage of participants with hSBA titers ≥ LLOQ for each N. meningitidis serogroup B
   indicator strains" — "At 1 month after the second dose of MenABCWY (Day 751 for the
   ABCWY-24 Group and Day 1471 for the ABCWY-48 Group)"
3. "Percentage of participants with solicited administration site events"
4. "Percentage of participants with solicited systemic events"
5. "Percentage of participants with any unsolicited adverse events (AEs) including all
   serious adverse events (SAEs), AEs leading to withdrawal, adverse events of special
   interest (AESIs) and medically attended AEs"
6. "Percentage of participants with SAEs, AEs leading to withdrawal, AESIs and medically
   attended AEs" — 6 months after first vaccination
7. Same measure — 6 months after second vaccination

The supporting description for the immunogenicity endpoints, verbatim:

> "The immune response to 2 doses of the MenABCWY vaccine administered on a 0-, 24-month
> schedule, and a 0-, 48-month schedule is evaluated against each N. meningitidis serogroup
> B indicator strains."

Note the verb in the official title: "**to Describe** the Safety, Tolerability, and
Immunogenicity". The endpoints are descriptive percentages, not a powered non-inferiority
or superiority comparison against a licensed schedule.

### Status

ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING; start 2021-11-18 (actual); primary completion and completion both
**2027-03-10 (ESTIMATED)**.

**Pricing implication:** a descriptive Phase 2b with two active schedules, no 0/6-month
comparator and no inferential primary endpoint cannot by itself support a 0/6-month
schedule claim. If the row assumes this study delivers that label change, the row is
mis-specified — the readout can happen on time and still not produce the claim being
priced. Whatever supports a schedule claim has to come from a different study.
